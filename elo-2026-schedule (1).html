Electronic literature does not stay inside the page.

The annual ELO conference brings together artists, scholars, writers, designers, and programmers working across digital and computational forms. ELO 2026 runs fully online, July 15–18, hosted by UCF. The theme, (un)supervised, asks who is watching, who is reading, and who is learning.
